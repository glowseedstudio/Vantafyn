Audiophile DSP Engine

6,000+ AutoEQ Headphone Calibrations

Vantafyn brings the comprehensive AutoEq database directly to your playback pipeline. Apply scientifically measured compensation curves to match the neutral Harman target curve with zero latency.

  • ✦ World-Class Measurement Data: Powered by Oratory1990, Crinacle, Rtings, and Innerfidelity research.
  • ✦ Hardware-Level 10-Band EQ: Applied directly via Android hardware audio session effects for zero artifacting.
  • ✦ Animated Squiggly Wave Scrubber: Dynamic wave kinetics during active playback that gently settle to a calm linear bar when paused.
  • ✦ ReplayGain Loudness Normalization: Smooth volume consistency across diverse albums and genres with anti-clipping headroom.
